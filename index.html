AI-Powered Quoting Engine — case summary
  • Problem: Dealer RFQs arrived incomplete and inconsistent, making quotes slow and error-prone.
  • Approach: Codified hard quoting rules (tax, trade-ins, configuration checks), connected CRM product lookup, and automated PDF delivery.
  • Result: A live system producing production-quality quotes for internal staff and external dealer reps.
Weighted TBE Scorecards — case summary
  • Problem: Engineering needed a fair, repeatable way to compare competing capital-equipment bids.
  • Approach: Weighted criteria across technical, compliance, service and commercial factors, with plain-language definitions.
  • Result: Transparent vendor comparisons that cross-functional teams could review and sign off on.
ELN Vendor Selection — case summary
  • Problem: R&D needed a compliant electronic lab notebook that matched real user workflows.
  • Approach: Gathered and refined 63 requirements with end users; tracked open RFI questions per vendor.
  • Result: A structured, auditable shortlist ready for QA/IT review and final selection.
